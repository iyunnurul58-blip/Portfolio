Omnichannel O2O Case Study

Sociolla Click & Collect Feature Enhancement

Redesigning the online-to-offline shopping journey to deliver real-time inventory visibility and instant order confirmation under 10 minutes.

📅 Duration: Aug 2026 – Jan 2027
🎯 Role: Project Manager

Core Project Management Competencies

Agile Scrum Delivery BRD / PRD / FSD Engineering O2O Process Optimization (BPMN) API Gateway & System Integration A/B Testing & Usability Validation Cross-Functional Stakeholder Alignment
Problem & Current Situation

⚡ Expectation vs Reality

Expectation: Instant pickup experience right after selecting product and store.

Reality: Confirmation takes 2–4 hours due to manual WhatsApp coordination between HQ Admin & Store Leaders.

Impact: High frustration for shoppers in mall environments, causing channel switching to competitors.

📊 Data Evidence

  • 65% Drop-off Rate at store selection & stock confirmation stage.
  • 183 mins avg wait time for Gen Z users in malls (leading to abandoned orders).
  • Qualitative Insight: "Mending langsung beli ke toko sebelah yang nggak perlu nunggu konfirmasi lama."

🔴 Problem Statement

Click & Collect cannot deliver a fast O2O experience because stock verification relies on manual HQ–WhatsApp–Store checks. This causes a 2–4 hour delay and a 65% drop-off rate among urgent mall shoppers.

Project Objectives & Targets (OKRs)

< 10 Mins

Target Order Confirmation SLA (Down from 2–4 Hours)

🎯 Strategic Solution Goals

  • Automate end-to-end order confirmation without HQ manual WhatsApp intervention.
  • Synchronize real-time store inventory directly with Product Detail Pages (PDP).
  • Provide a store fulfillment dashboard for Store Leaders to process orders within 15 mins.

Solution & Usability Outcomes

💡 Implemented Key Solutions

  • Home Entry Point: Prominent Click & Collect banner on Home Page.
  • Upfront Real-Time Stock: Available/Out of stock badges directly on PDP.
  • Instant Auto-Confirmation: SLA <10 minutes auto-approval.
  • Live Order Tracker: Step-by-step status updates with QR verification.

📈 Usability Testing Results

  • Task Completion Rate: 100% across participants.
  • Single Ease Question (SEQ): Improved from 2.4 (Control) to 1.0 (Very Easy).
  • Misclicks: Reduced from 5 misclicks to 1 misclick.
  • User Intent: High purchase confidence due to stock transparency.
